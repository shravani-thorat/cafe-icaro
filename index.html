Our story

A song whispered to the soul

An icaro is a healing chant, born in the heart of the Amazon and carried by healers through the stillness of ayahuasca nights. This café borrows that spirit: a place to exhale.

Nestled amidst rustling chikoo and coconut trees, Icaro is a collaboration with 729 Grams Coffee. Here, time slows down. Sip a cup of thoughtfully brewed specialty coffee and savour an all-day menu crafted for comfort, flavour and slow living.
